Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is this platform?
Our platform is a comprehensive solution for managing projects, documents, and team collaboration. It combines powerful features with an intuitive interface to help teams work more efficiently.

How much does it cost?
We offer several pricing tiers:
- Free: Up to 3 users, 5GB storage
- Professional: $10/user/month, 100GB storage
- Enterprise: Custom pricing, unlimited storage

Is there a free trial?
Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial of our Professional plan with no credit card required. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

Account and Billing

How do I upgrade my account?
1. Go to Account Settings > Billing
2. Select your desired plan
3. Enter payment information
4. Confirm the upgrade

Changes take effect immediately, and you'll be prorated for the current billing period.

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your account will remain active until the end of the current billing period, and no further charges will be made.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept:
- Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
- PayPal
- Bank transfer (for Enterprise plans)
- Purchase orders (for Enterprise plans)

Features and Functionality

Can I import data from other tools?
Yes! We support imports from:
- CSV files
- Excel spreadsheets
- Other project management tools (contact support)
- Cloud storage services

Export capabilities include CSV, PDF, and JSON formats.

Is there a mobile app?
Yes, we offer native mobile apps for iOS and Android with full offline functionality. Download from the App Store or Google Play.

Can multiple people work on the same document?
Yes, our platform supports real-time collaboration. Multiple users can edit simultaneously, with changes synced instantly and conflicts automatically resolved.

Security and Privacy

Is my data secure?
We take security seriously:
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- SOC 2 Type II certified
- GDPR compliant
- Regular security audits

Where is data stored?
Data is stored in secure data centers with multiple redundancy. You can choose your data region during account setup. We offer:
- US East
- US West
- EU (Frankfurt)
- Asia Pacific (Singapore)

Who can see my data?
Only you and team members you explicitly invite can access your data. Our staff cannot access your data except in rare support cases with your explicit permission.

Do you backup my data?
Yes, we perform:
- Continuous backups throughout the day
- Daily snapshots retained for 30 days
- Weekly snapshots retained for 1 year

You can also export and backup your data at any time.

Technical Questions

What browsers are supported?
We support the latest versions of:
- Google Chrome (recommended)
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari
- Microsoft Edge

Internet Explorer is not supported.

What are the system requirements?
Minimum requirements:
- Modern web browser
- Internet connection (1 Mbps minimum)
- JavaScript enabled
- Cookies enabled

For optimal performance, we recommend:
- 10 Mbps or faster internet
- 8GB RAM
- Modern computer (within 5 years)

Can I use it offline?
The mobile apps support offline mode. Desktop access requires an internet connection, but drafts are saved locally and synced when reconnected.
