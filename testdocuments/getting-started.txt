Getting Started with Our Platform

Welcome to our comprehensive guide for new users. This document will walk you through the essential steps to begin using our platform effectively.

Installation

To install our platform, follow these simple steps:

1. Download the installer from our official website
2. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions
3. Enter your license key when prompted
4. Complete the installation wizard

System Requirements

Before installing, ensure your system meets these minimum requirements:

- Operating System: Windows 10 or later, macOS 10.15+, or Linux (Ubuntu 20.04+)
- RAM: 8GB minimum, 16GB recommended
- Disk Space: 2GB available space
- Internet Connection: Required for activation and updates

First Steps

After installation, follow these initial steps:

1. Launch the application
2. Sign in with your account credentials
3. Complete the initial setup wizard
4. Familiarize yourself with the dashboard

The dashboard provides quick access to all major features and displays your recent activity.

Basic Navigation

The main interface consists of three primary areas:

- Left Sidebar: Navigation menu for accessing different modules
- Central Panel: Main workspace where you perform tasks
- Right Panel: Properties and settings for selected items

You can customize the layout by dragging panels to your preferred positions.

Need Help?

If you encounter any issues during setup, please refer to our troubleshooting guide or contact our support team at support@example.com.
